Goal: replicate **std0** (`0xdf7930e89a2c47560165331863c31deca0733dcd`), whose profit is

## TL;DR — the one fact that changes everything

**Polymarket runs TWO separate, unrelated reward systems. std0 earns from the SECOND one,
and crypto Up/Down markets are in ONLY the second one.**

| | (A) **Liquidity Rewards** (Qmin) | (B) **Maker Rebates** (fee share) |
|---|---|---|
| What it pays for | Resting orders *close to mid*, sampled per minute, scored `S=((v−s)/v)²·b` | Resting orders that **get FILLED** |
| Pool | **Fixed sponsor pool**, `rate_per_day` $/market (CLOB `/rewards/markets`) | **20 % of the taker fees** that market actually collects that day |
| Driver | size × closeness-to-mid × two-sidedness × uptime | **filled volume × p(1−p)** |
| On crypto Up/Down? | **NO** — 0 of 4 500 reward-pool markets are crypto-updown; `/rewards/markets/{cond}` → `count=0` | **YES** — `feeSchedule.rebateRate=0.2`, `makerRebatesFeeShareBps=10000` |
| Activity-feed type | (n/a here) | `MAKER_REBATE` / `TAKER_REBATE` |

> **Correction to `std0_strategy.md`:** std0's income is NOT the "size × closeness-to-mid ×
> uptime" Qmin score (that program doesn't touch crypto Up/Down). It is **20 % of the taker
> fees generated by its own filled maker orders**, weighted by `p(1−p)`. The `rewardsMinSize:50`
> / `rewardsMaxSpread:4.5` fields on btc-updown are **vestigial Qmin defaults that pay $0** —
> there is no Qmin pool on these markets. The binding lever is **filled maker volume near
> p≈0.5**, not closeness to the mid for a per-minute score.

## 1. How the two programs work

### (A) Liquidity Rewards (Qmin) — the classic LP program (NOT used by crypto Up/Down)
Documented formula (kept here for completeness / other markets):

- Per resting order, per minute snapshot: **`S(v,s) = ((v − s)/v)² · b`**
  - `v` = market's **max spread** (`rewards_max_spread`, in **cents** from the mid),
  - `s` = the order's **actual spread** from the (size-cutoff-adjusted) midpoint, in cents,
  - `b` = the order **size** (shares). Quadratic ⇒ closeness to mid is heavily rewarded;
    an order at the band edge scores ≈0.
- Only orders **≥ `rewards_min_size` shares** and **within `v` cents of mid** score.
- Two sides: `Q_one` = Σ scores of {bids on token m + asks on m'}; `Q_two` = the mirror.
  - midpoint ∈ **[0.10, 0.90]**: `Q_min = max( min(Q_one,Q_two), max(Q_one/c, Q_two/c) )`
    → single-sided allowed at **1/c**, with **`c = 3.0`** currently.
  - midpoint ∈ **[0, 0.10) ∪ (0.90, 1.0]**: `Q_min = min(Q_one, Q_two)` → **must be two-sided**.
- Sampled **every minute, random offset**; `Q_epoch = Σ Q_min over the epoch (10 080 one-minute
  samples ≈ 1 week) × uptime`; **`Q_final = Q_epoch / Σ(all makers' Q_epoch)`**.
- Payout = `Q_final × market's daily reward pool`, paid **daily at ~00:00 UTC**, **$1 minimum**.
- Pools are per-market `rate_per_day` (live histogram: most $1–$10/day, a long tail to $5 000/day).

### (B) Maker Rebates — fee redistribution (THIS is std0's engine)
- Crypto markets charge a **taker-only fee**: `fee = feeRate · p·(1−p) · shares`, with
  **`feeRate = 0.07`** (`feeSchedule = {rate:0.07, exponent:1, takerOnly:true}`). Makers pay 0.
- A fraction of those collected taker fees is redistributed to makers:
  - **`fee_equivalent = C · feeRate · p · (1−p)`** (C = shares filled),
  - **`rebate = (your_fee_equivalent / total_fee_equivalent) · rebate_pool`**,
  - **`rebate_pool = rebateRate × taker fees collected`**, **`rebateRate = 0.20` for Crypto**
    (`makerRebatesFeeShareBps = 10000` ⇒ 100 % of that pool goes to makers).
  - Category table: **Crypto 20 %**; Sports/Finance/Politics/Econ/Culture/Weather/Tech/
    Mentions/Other **25 %**; Geopolitics fee-free.
- **Net effect (the number to internalize):** the maker on a filled order earns
  **`0.20 × 0.07 × p(1−p) = 0.014 · p(1−p)` USDC per filled share.**
- Eligibility = "post resting orders that get filled." **No band, no min-size, no Qmin** — a
  fill anywhere in the book qualifies; the only weighting is `p(1−p)` (max at p=0.5).
- **Paid daily in USDC, ~midnight UTC, $1 minimum**, as a **wallet-level aggregate** (not per-market).

`TAKER_REBATE` also appears on std0 (~$350–600/day): a taker-side rebate on the same crypto
fee (not documented in the maker-rebates help article; observed empirically, paid the same
daily cadence). Maker is the dominant stream; treat taker rebate as a smaller bonus.

## 2. Eligibility — can our wallet just quote and earn? **Yes.**

- Docs: *"Place orders that add liquidity to the book and get filled."* **No registration,
  no opt-in, no KYC, no builder/program enrollment, no minimum-size-to-qualify** for the
  rebate. Rebates are computed automatically from on-chain fills and paid to the
  `maker_address` (the proxy wallet that owns the order).
- Our wallet `0x32D30125710521fF3Bf174d6440374Cb39634029` (sig_type 3 Polymarket deposit/proxy,
  signer EOA `0x9C9A11397E70e2560145c8EDdeaC90209B008f15`) is **architecturally identical** to
  std0's proxy. sig_type is irrelevant to rebate eligibility. **It qualifies by simply quoting
  crypto Up/Down and getting filled.** (For the Qmin program you'd also need a market that has
  a pool — crypto Up/Down doesn't — so that path is moot here.)

## 5. The exact recipe to earn

**You are paid `0.014 · p(1−p)` USDC per filled maker share. Maximize FILLED maker volume,
preferentially while the price is near 0.5.** Closeness-to-mid only matters insofar as it gets
you filled (top of book); it is *not* independently scored here.

Per-filled-share rebate and the volume needed for a target income (assuming you're the
dominant maker, i.e. capture ≈100 % like std0; otherwise scale by your fee-equivalent share):

| mid price p | rebate/share = 0.014·p(1−p) | shares/day for $1 000 | ≈ notional/day @ that p |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.50 | $0.00350 | 286 k | ~$143 k |
| 0.65 | $0.00319 | 314 k | ~$188 k |
| 0.75 | $0.00263 | 381 k | ~$286 k |
| 0.85 | $0.00179 | 559 k | ~$475 k |
| 0.90 | $0.00126 | 794 k | ~$715 k |

So **$1 000/day in maker rebates ≈ 300 k filled shares/day near p≈0.65 ≈ ~$190 k/day of filled
maker notional ≈ ~$8 k/h** — consistent with std0's measured ~12 k shares/h. (This is *flow*,
not capital at risk: the inventory is minted from sets and redeemed at par each window; see
`std0_ops.md`.)

**Concrete quoting loop (per `<coin>-<frame>` window; weight capital to btc-5m then btc-15m):**
1. **Mint ammunition** ~50 s before open: `SPLIT` ~2 500 sets on btc, ~1 000 on alts → balanced
   Up+Down inventory to post resting size on both sides. (Direct-EOA tx, pipelined nonces — see
   `std0_ops.md`; recovered at par via `REDEEM` ~44 s after close.)
2. **Quote two-sided, get filled, in the FIRST HALF of the window** (where both `p(1−p)` *and*
   taker volume peak — this is *why* std0 front-loads): post resting **bids and asks on both Up
   and Down**, tight enough to sit at/near top of book (std0's posted full spread ≈ 4.3¢ btc →
   7.4¢ xrp; half-spread each side), in small **$3–5 clips**, **re-pegged every 1–6 s** to the
   moving mid (anchor the fair value to Binance spot). The fee-rebate doesn't require any
   specific band — but you must actually be **lifted/hit** to earn, so stay competitive.
3. **Stay roughly delta-neutral**; lean quotes against inventory; **pull all orders ~30–60 s
   before settlement** (avoid late, informed adverse fills — the rebate isn't worth getting run
   over). std0 ends every window long the balanced minted block ⇒ **zero short risk**.
4. **Redeem** the winning side after close → recovers the mint; net cash = spread captured.

**Economics to expect:** raw spread P&L is thin (std0 ≈ break-even on btc-5m, +$/win only on
btc-15m). **The income is the rebate**: design for *fill volume*, not spread. At std0's size
(~300 k filled shares/day, ~$190 k/day flow) that is **~$1k–1.8k/day**. Linear in your filled
volume × your fee-equivalent share — so the real constraints are (i) how much resting size you
can keep filled at the top of book, (ii) how many makers you split the 20 % pool with
(std0 currently has little competition on btc-5m), and (iii) adverse selection on fills, which
the −60 s cutoff and tiny clips control.

## Open items / caveats
- **`TAKER_REBATE` mechanism** isn't in the public maker-rebates article; it's an observed
  taker-side rebate (~$350–600/day for std0) on the same 0.07 crypto fee. Treat as a bonus.
- The **Qmin formula** (§1A) is included for completeness and applies if we ever make markets
  that *do* carry a fixed pool (most non-crypto markets) — but it is **irrelevant to crypto
  Up/Down**, which pay purely via the fee rebate.
- Code note: repo currently has `FEE_RATE=0` in places; the fee **is real (0.07 taker)** and is
  what funds the rebate — relevant if we model net economics of our own taker crosses.
- Rebate share is **pro-rata across makers**; our earnings = std0-like only if we win a similar
  share of filled volume. More competition compresses both spread and rebate share.
